Include registration evidence

Inferred preparation guidance: record whether scan stations share one registered frame. Include the registration report, station list and transforms when available. Identify separate unregistered scans. Name the capture date, equipment and any filtering or resampling.

Inferred acceptance guidance: registration residuals describe the registration check used. They do not establish independent model accuracy. Identify independent control observations separately. Mark missing checks as unknown.

Describe the coordinate system

Inferred scope guidance: state horizontal and vertical reference systems, units, origin and axis orientation. Supply an EPSG identifier when applicable. For a local frame, describe its origin and relation to site control. Include any translation, rotation or scale applied during export.

Inferred review guidance: agree whether the deliverable keeps survey coordinates or uses a local modelling origin. Record the transformation between them. A screenshot alone cannot define the coordinate frame.
