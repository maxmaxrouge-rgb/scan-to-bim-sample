What does an LOD 200 or LOD 300 scan-to-BIM deliverable include?

LOD describes element development

Verified (fetched reference): the BIMForum 2019 LOD Specification describes LOD 200 as generic elements with approximate geometry. LOD 300 describes specific elements whose quantity, size, shape, location and orientation can be measured from the model.

Inferred application to existing buildings: agree an element schedule and evidence requirements alongside the chosen specification edition. An LOD label alone does not establish measured agreement with the building or a numerical survey tolerance.

The sample IFC exposes the evidence gaps

Verified (fetched sample and QA): the sample IFC contains 7 IfcWall elements and 5 IfcSlab elements. The QA page identifies classifications as inferred and thickness as illustrative at 0.1 m. Convex hull extents can bridge gaps.

Inferred worked example: a wall plane supports a candidate surface. It does not independently establish the hidden wall face, actual thickness, full boundary or an opening. An illustrative extrusion cannot prove those properties.

Inferred assessment: the generic planes illustrate questions relevant to an LOD 200 brief. This guide assigns no certified LOD to the sample. An LOD 300 request would need agreed element requirements and supporting evidence beyond these illustrative assumptions.

Inferred review method: compare the browser viewer with the QA limitations and IFC inventory. Record which requested properties lack evidence. Browser appearance and plane-fit residuals do not establish surveyed accuracy or construction suitability.
